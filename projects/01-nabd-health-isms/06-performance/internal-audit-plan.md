## 1. Purpose

This plan defines the internal audit of Nabd Health's ISMS against ISO/IEC 27001:2022,
the organization's own requirements, and applicable legal and contractual obligations.
It satisfies clause 9.2 and provides the basis for certification readiness.

## 2. Audit objectives

1. Determine whether the ISMS conforms to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 clauses 4 to 10 and the
   applicable Annex A controls.
2. Determine whether the ISMS is effectively implemented and maintained.
3. Verify that risk treatment and the Statement of Applicability are consistent.
4. Identify nonconformities, observations, and opportunities for improvement.
5. Provide management with a basis for certification decisions.

## 7. Methods

- Interviews with process owners and sampled staff.
- Document and record review.
- Sampling of access reviews, offboarding, changes, incidents, backups, and supplier
  assessments.
- Technical verification of MFA, logging, encryption, backup configuration, and staging
  data.
- Observation of physical controls at L-01.
- Traceability checks from risk to control to evidence.

Sampling is risk-based. A sample failure is investigated to determine whether it is
isolated or systemic. Findings are classified as major nonconformity, minor
nonconformity, observation, or opportunity for improvement.

## 8. Reporting and follow-up

- Findings are discussed with the process owner before the closing meeting.
- The audit report is issued within 10 business days.
- Corrective actions are recorded in NH-NC-001 with owners and due dates.
- The auditor verifies closure of nonconformities and reports to the CEO.
- Results are an input to management review and the certification readiness decision.

## 9. Confidentiality and access

Audit evidence is stored in a restricted folder. The auditor has read-only access to
systems and records for the audit period. Personal data is minimized in working papers.
Audit access is logged under A.8.34.

## 10. Risks to the audit

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Key staff unavailable | Deputies named; interviews rescheduled within the audit window |
| Evidence incomplete | Evidence index and owners; gaps reported as findings |
| Auditor independence challenged | Contracted auditor with no operational role; signed declaration |
| Scope drift | Scope confirmed at opening meeting against NH-CTX-003 |
