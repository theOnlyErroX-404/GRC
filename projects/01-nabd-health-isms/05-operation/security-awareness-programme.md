> ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation and does not describe a real organization.

## 1. Purpose

This programme builds the security knowledge and behaviour required by NH-POL-004 and
A.6.3. It focuses on the risks that matter most to Nabd: phishing, patient-data handling,
credential hygiene, incident reporting, and secure remote working.

## 2. Audience and curriculum

| Audience | Mandatory modules | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| All staff and contractors | Security essentials; phishing and social engineering; data classification and handling; incident reporting; remote working; privacy and PDPL basics | Onboarding and quarterly refresher |
| Support and operations | Patient-data handling; Admin Panel access; ticket redaction; customer impersonation and verification | Onboarding and twice yearly |
| Engineering | Secure coding; secrets management; dependency and supply-chain risk; secure code review; AI coding tool rules | Onboarding and annually |
| Executives | Incident roles; regulatory obligations; decision-making in a crisis; business email compromise | Annually |
| HR and finance | Payment fraud; employee data protection; supplier due diligence | Annually |
| Contractors (Pixelwave) | Security essentials; code and access rules; incident reporting; device requirements | Onboarding and annually |

## 3. Delivery

- Short online modules with a knowledge check (80 percent pass mark).
- Live sessions for engineering and support, recorded for new starters.
- Monthly phishing simulations with immediate teachable moments.
- Quarterly security newsletter with real (anonymized) lessons from incidents and audit
  findings.
- Posters and desktop reminders for reporting and clear desk.

Training is delivered in English with Arabic summaries for key messages where useful.

## 4. Phishing simulations

| Month | Theme | Difficulty | Click rate | Report rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| January 2027 | Invoice attachment | Standard | 8.1 percent | 62 percent |
| February 2027 | Password reset | Standard | 5.3 percent | 71 percent |
| March 2027 | Shared document | Hard | 3.4 percent | 78 percent |

Anyone who clicks receives a short learning page. Repeat clickers receive coaching from
their manager and, after three failures in six months, additional training. Results are
reported without naming individuals.

## 5. Completion tracking

HR maintains completion records in the learning management system. The ISMS Manager
reviews completion monthly and reports to the CTO. New starters must complete the first
module within five working days. Non-completion is escalated to the line manager and
recorded as a KPI exception.

Targets:

- 100 percent completion for mandatory modules.
- 95 percent completion within the assigned window.
- Phishing click rate below 5 percent and report rate above 70 percent by Q3 2027.

## 6. Content maintenance

Content is reviewed annually and after:

- a significant incident or near miss;
- an audit finding related to human behaviour;
- a change in policy, law, or technology;
- a new phishing technique observed in the sector.

The ISMS Manager and HR Manager approve updates. Training records are retained for three
years.

## 7. Effectiveness

Effectiveness is measured through completion, phishing simulation results, incident
reporting rates, helpdesk questions, and audit findings. A quarterly report is included
in NH-KPI-001 and management review. Training is adjusted when metrics show persistent
weakness.
