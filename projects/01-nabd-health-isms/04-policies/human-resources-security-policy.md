> ISO/IEC 27001:2022 implementation and does not describe a real organization.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines security requirements across the employment lifecycle: screening,
terms of employment, awareness, discipline, confidentiality, remote working, and
responsibilities after termination. It implements Annex A controls A.6.1 to A.6.8.

## 2. Scope

Applies to employees, interns, and contractors, including Pixelwave developers working
on Nabd systems. Supplier organizations are covered by NH-POL-007.

## 3. Screening

| Role type | Checks before access |
|---|---|
| All employees | Identity verification, CV verification, two reference checks, right-to-work check |
| Finance and administrators | Above, plus criminal record check where lawful |
| Engineering and production access | Above, plus verification of qualifications and previous employment |
| Contractors | Identity verification, reference checks, contractor company confirmation, signed NDA |

Results are recorded by HR and retained for the employment period plus the retention
period in NH-POL-011. Where a check cannot be completed, access is limited until it is
resolved and the decision is documented.

## 4. Terms and conditions

Employment contracts and contractor agreements must include:

- confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations;
- the requirement to comply with Nabd policies and complete awareness training;
- responsibilities for information and assets;
- intellectual property assignment for work created for Nabd;
- the disciplinary process and consequences of violation;
- obligations after termination.

Contractors must also be covered by a services agreement with the security schedule in
NH-POL-007.

## 5. Onboarding

Before access is granted:

1. HR confirms the signed contract, screening completion, and NDA.
2. The line manager defines the role and required access.
3. IT creates accounts and issues a managed device.
4. The new starter completes security awareness training and acknowledges the policies.
5. The line manager records completion.

Access is not granted before these steps are complete, except for a documented
pre-start requirement approved by the CTO.

## 6. Awareness and training

- All staff complete security awareness training at onboarding and quarterly thereafter.
- Engineering staff complete secure coding training annually.
- Support staff complete patient-data handling and social engineering training.
- Phishing simulations run monthly; repeated failure triggers targeted coaching.
- Completion is tracked by HR and reported in NH-KPI-001.

Details are in NH-PRC-006.

## 7. Disciplinary process

Violations are handled under the employee handbook. Factors include intent, harm,
recurrence, and whether the issue was self-reported. Sanctions range from coaching to
termination and, where appropriate, referral to authorities. Contractors are managed
under the supplier agreement.

## 8. Remote working

Remote working is governed by NH-POL-013. Managers must confirm that the employee has a
suitable private space, a managed device, and reliable connectivity before approving
remote work involving patient data.

## 9. Termination and change of role

HR must:

1. notify IT and the line manager at least one week before a planned departure, or
   immediately for a dismissal;
2. ensure access is removed on or before the last working day;
3. recover devices, fobs, and documents using the asset return checklist;
4. conduct an exit interview covering confidentiality and continuing obligations;
5. confirm completion to the ISMS Manager.

For internal moves, the new manager must confirm the new access required and the old
access to remove. Access is updated within five working days.

Post-employment obligations continue for the periods defined in the contract,
particularly confidentiality and the prohibition on retaining Nabd data.

## 10. Reporting and support

Staff can report security concerns through the incident channel or HR. Reports are
handled confidentially, and retaliation against good-faith reporting is prohibited.
Support is available through the employee assistance arrangements described in the
handbook.
